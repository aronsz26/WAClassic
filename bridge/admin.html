1 Install WA Classic

On the jailbroken iPhone open Cydia, tap Sources › Edit › Add and enter:

http://cydia.waclassic.com

Then tap WA Classic in the new source and install it. Cydia shows the right version for your iOS. Updates come from there too.

No internet on the iPhone, or the source doesn't load? This bridge is a source too:

The look of WhatsApp 2.10

WA Classic doesn't come with WhatsApp's own pictures and sounds. The bridge can download the old WhatsApp app once from the Internet Archive, keep only its pictures and sounds, and hand them to the iPhone: WhatsApp 2.10.2 (10 MB) for the look of iOS 4 to 6.

3 Link WhatsApp

After step 2 the iPhone shows a QR code. On your phone open WhatsApp, tap Settings › Linked Devices › Link a Device and scan the code on the iPhone.

4 Away from home optional

The bridge runs without its TLS port, so it only works at home.

Only needed if you want WA Classic on mobile data or in other Wi-Fis. At home it works without this.

  1. In your router, forward TCP port to this computer. (Only this port, never port .)
  2. Type your home's internet address below (a DynDNS name or a fixed IP) and click Save.
  3. Open WA Classic once while you're at home, it picks up the new address by itself.

Step by step, with router names and DynDNS: guide.waclassic.com/away-from-home

Security

Away from home the connection is encrypted. The iPhone only accepts this bridge's own certificate (SHA-256 ), and every request needs the key that the setup code hands out. Messages are stored unencrypted on this computer, so keep it to yourself.

Manage

Really unlink? The chats stored here are deleted.

Unlinking removes this bridge from your WhatsApp account and deletes the chats stored here. Every iPhone on this bridge is logged out. Afterwards you can link it again, also with another account.
